# First Tutorial Flow

> Historical implementation note: use `TUTORIAL_FLOW.md` for the current
> authoritative Day 1 order.

The game begins with the Farmer finding the player unconscious and bringing
them back to the homestead. The player wakes outside the cabin and receives the
opening greeting before free movement begins.

1. Complete the rescue/wake-up opening and Farmer greeting.
2. Talk to the Farmer's Wife.
3. Follow the guidance arrow to the Task Board and accept `Fieldstone by Hand`.
4. Collect six small Fieldstones. The Farmer walks over, explains the faith
   notches in his axe handle, gives the axe, and then the player claims the task.
5. Accept `A Fallen Tree`, fell and inspect a tree, then claim the task.
6. Accept `Stone Haul`, crush ten Fieldrocks, deliver all sixteen Fieldstones,
   and claim the task to make the winch attachment arrive.
7. Accept `Fit the Winch`, collect and install the attachment, then claim it.
8. Accept `Timber Delivery`, haul the log and stump into Home Delivery, then
   claim it to finish `A Firm Foundation`.
9. Accept `Park the Skidsteer` under `A Place of Your Own`; park on the pad
   beside the Farmer, stop, detach any tow, exit, and claim it.
10. Accept `Mark the Cabin Site`; take a flag at authored Site I or Site II and
    claim it.
11. Accept `Build the Cabin Boundary`; mill planks and fence pieces, collect
    Finished Crafts, and fill the authored fence/gate blueprint sockets.
12. Accept `Build the Cabin` and raise it. The player says they are thirsty,
    and the Farmer walks up to praise the day's work and explain one last job.
13. Make one Empty Bucket at the unlocked lathe, collect it from Finished
    Crafts, fill it at the pond, and pour it into the authored water tank. The
    tank starts at `10/40`; the first bucket changes it to `11/40`.
14. Claim the cabin task and rest to enter Day 2. Rest remains locked until the
    first water delivery is complete.

The normal yellow marker sits above an objective while it is inside the camera.
When the objective is outside the camera, a labeled yellow arrow at the screen
edge points in its direction.

Turning in the log advances the active timber task to the stump objective.
Delivering the stump as Small Lumber completes the task; claiming it at the
board completes Quest 1 and unlocks the short cabin quest.

The first six hand-pickable Fieldstones prefer registered spawn-area positions,
with nearby one-time stones used only as a fallback. The `obj_fieldrock`
resources are large, skidsteer-only material; crushing ten of them supplies the
remaining Fieldstones needed for the 16-stone cabin target.

Press `I` or `Tab` to open the Inventory menu. Its Backpack, Vehicle, Homebase,
and Tools categories read the live inventories and persistent unlock state. Press
`Q` separately for the Quest Journal.
